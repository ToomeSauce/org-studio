⚠ The kill case ALWAYS FIRST · CANNOT COLLAPSE

The strongest argument this fails: the pain is real but the felt cost is near zero. Freelancers tolerate late invoices; they send one email and move on. Products that monetize tolerated pain need either a moment of acute crisis (none here) or bundling into a tool already owned (FreshBooks and Bonsai already do this as a featureSOURCED ↗). A standalone subscription asks users to pay monthly for a problem they feel quarterly.

What would prove this wrong: 25+ interviews where freelancers describe a lost client or cashflow crisis from a late invoice in the past 6 months, or a waitlist conversion above 8%MODELED ⌄ from a fake-door test.
